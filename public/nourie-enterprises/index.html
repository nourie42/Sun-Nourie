FUEL DEAL PRESSURE TEST

There is more to a fuel deal than the price on the invoice.

These are examples of the commercial questions a dealer-side review can surface. Actual priorities depend on the agreement and state law.

01What is my true delivered cost? +

Compare rack references or DTW prices, freight, taxes, prompt-pay terms, credits, rebates and other applicable fees on a consistent per-gallon basis.

02What does the agreement obligate me to buy? +

Model minimum volumes, shortfall costs, product sourcing limits and other obligations under plausible operating scenarios.

03What happens when the term ends? +

Identify business assumptions affected by term length, renewal notice, branding investments, unamortized incentives and potential transition costs. Legal rights must be reviewed by counsel.

04Am I receiving the full incentive value? +

Compare upfront funding with ongoing purchase economics and obligations so the long-run cost of the agreement is visible.

05Would another structure work better? +

Compare commercial alternatives over the same volume, capital, delivery and ownership assumptions rather than judging offers by only one headline number.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Clear answers.
Before big decisions.

Who would this service be designed for?+

Independent convenience-store operators, especially owners of one to ten locations who want experienced commercial analysis without building an in-house finance team.

Would this company work for fuel suppliers?+

The concept is dealer-paid advisory services without supplier-paid placement commissions, designed to preserve independence and transparent incentives.

Is a fuel agreement review legal advice?+

No. Commercial modeling, benchmarking and negotiation preparation are distinct from legal opinions. Any interpretation of legal rights or contract enforceability should be performed by qualified counsel.

Can I submit my fuel agreement or profit reports here?+

Not yet. This is a concept preview and has no file intake, account registration or customer information collection. Please do not submit confidential documents through a public website.

How much would services cost?+

Potential pilot packages include a one-time store review, fuel economics analysis and monthly advisory retainer. Pricing, scope and availability are not final and will be validated before launch.
