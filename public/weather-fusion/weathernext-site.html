Know what you are looking at

What is included—and what is not

Google's BigQuery feed offers 19 gridded surface variables and two station-trained fields, each with mean, P10, P25, P50, P75 and P90 statistics. Available fields are discovered from the linked tables. This page never fills missing fields with another model or an invented estimate.

Main 00/06/12/18 UTC runs can extend to 360 forecast hours. Newer interim runs can extend to 48 hours. They are selectable separately: different runs are not silently joined. Model initialization, publication and the site's collection time are different timestamps.

The published locations are listed above. This is not an arbitrary-location global point API. Station-trained output is still a forecast—not a current station reading. Wind gusts, live radar, official warnings, UV index and a calibrated probability of rain are not supplied by this surface-statistics feed.

Raw 64-member trajectories, pressure-level fields and native six-hour accumulation products use Google's separate Cloud Storage feed. They are not reconstructed from percentiles here. Use the official resources below for those products and global forecast maps.
