Models, timing & forecast policy

Now & near term

NWS observations and official hourly guidance, with HRRR for short-range precipitation detail. Radar shows observed reflectivity, not a forecast.

The week ahead

NWS remains primary. NOAA’s National Blend and ECMWF IFS offer comparison and fallback guidance. Agreement is not a calibrated accuracy score.

Precipitation accounting

Totals use local 7 AM–7 AM windows, including overnight weather. These are full-window forecast estimates, not measured rain. Snow and ice are included as liquid equivalent.

Open-Meteo supplies explicitly selected model data, not its automatic “Best Match” forecast. Retrieval timestamps are not model initialization times. Exact run provenance is not supplied in these point-forecast responses. Separate Windy maps show the provider’s model, not the local Weather Fusion calculation.

The optional HRRR 60% / ECMWF 40% comparison is an illustrative, uncalibrated blend and renormalizes only over complete source totals. It is not advertised as the most accurate forecast.
