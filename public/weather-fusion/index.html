Models, timing & forecast policy

Now & near term

NWS observations and official hourly guidance remain the numerical anchor. The local Area Forecast Discussion provides forecaster context, and the HRRR map is available for short-range convective timing and reflectivity guidance.

The week ahead

NWS remains primary. NOAA’s National Blend and ECMWF IFS are available as direct model-map references for comparison. Agreement between maps is context, not a calibrated accuracy score.

Precipitation accounting

NWS grid totals use local 7 AM–7 AM windows, including overnight weather. These are full-window forecast estimates, not measured rain. Snow and ice are included as liquid equivalent.

Direct-source policy: Weather Fusion does not scrape Weather.us or treat it as a weather API. The HRRR and ECMWF buttons open the user-selected Weather.us visualizations you provided; official source links remain NOAA/NCEP and ECMWF. NBM links directly to NOAA/NCEP model guidance. The numerical public forecast continues to come from NWS unless a verified direct point-model decoder is available.

The optional HRRR 60% / ECMWF 40% comparison is not displayed as authoritative when direct point values are unavailable. Raw-model maps are guidance and may differ from the official forecast.
