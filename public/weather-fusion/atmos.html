The forecasts, blended into one number

Weather Nourie starts from the National Weather Service forecast and blends in computer models, each with a fixed share. The shaded band is how far apart they are. The white line is the blend you see everywhere on this site.

How it’s really gonna feel

Feels-like is the site’s outdoor UTCI estimate: air temperature, moisture, wind and an estimated sun load working together, with the NWS wind chill as a floor in cold wind. It describes the air around you, not your skin.

How the rain chance adds up

The rain chance here is a score. The NWS chance fills its share, and each model adds points only for the rain it actually forecasts: all of its points for real rain that something else backs up, a third for a trace.
