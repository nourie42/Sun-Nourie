    How Blend V2 & Outlier Dampening Work

    Weather Nourie shifts weights across five lead-time horizons: 0–24h (NWS 20%, HRRR 25%, NBM 25%, ECMWF 15%, WeatherNext 15%), 24–48h (NWS 15%, HRRR 20%, NBM 25%, ECMWF 20%, WeatherNext 20%), 48–72h (NWS 15%, NBM 25%, ECMWF 25%, GEFS 10%, WeatherNext 25%), Days 4–7 (NWS 10%, NBM 25%, ECMWF 25%, GEFS 10%, WeatherNext 30%), and Days 8–10 (NBM 10%, ECMWF 30%, GEFS 20%, WeatherNext 40%).

    How UTCI / Steadman Comfort & 2-Layer Pavement Heat Work

    Apparent temperature fuses UTCI with Steadman Apparent Temperature in mild weather (55–85°F), NWS Wind Chill below 60°F, and ASHRAE solar ERF in direct sun. Sidewalk concrete and asphalt heat are solved via a 2-layer energy-balance model evaluated against the 135°F AAHA pet paw-burn threshold.
