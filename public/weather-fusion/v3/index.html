    How Blend V2 & Outlier Dampening (`weather-nourie-temperature-outliers-v1`) Work

    Weather Nourie dynamically shifts temperature weights across five lead-time horizons: 0–24h (NWS 20%, HRRR 25%, NBM 25%, ECMWF 15%, WeatherNext 15%), 24–48h (NWS 15%, HRRR 20%, NBM 25%, ECMWF 20%, WeatherNext 20%), 48–72h (NWS 15%, NBM 25%, ECMWF 25%, GEFS 10%, WeatherNext 25%), Days 4–7 (NWS 10%, NBM 25%, ECMWF 25%, GEFS 10%, WeatherNext 30%), and Days 8–10 (NBM 10%, ECMWF 30%, GEFS 20%, WeatherNext 40%). On Days 1–2, if ECMWF diverges ≥4°F from the peer median, its weight is dampened to 10% and remaining feeds renormalize.

    How UTCI / Steadman Thermal Comfort & 2-Layer Pavement Heat Work

    Apparent temperature uses the Universal Thermal Climate Index (UTCI) as its physical baseline, transitioning smoothly into Steadman Apparent Temperature in mild temperatures (55–85°F) and fading toward NWS Wind Chill below 60°F. Direct sun adds ASHRAE standing-person Effective Radiant Field (ERF) from shortwave solar irradiance. Concrete and dark asphalt temperatures are integrated via a 2-layer energy-balance model over 48 hours and evaluated against the 135°F AAHA pet paw-burn threshold.
