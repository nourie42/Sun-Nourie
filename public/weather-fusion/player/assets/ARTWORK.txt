Shared master brief:
Photorealistic natural DSLR-style photograph of one adult brunette woman in a
leafy North Carolina-style lakeside park. Natural human proportions, skin,
hair strands, fabric weave, foliage and cloud detail. Low camera angle with
broad overhead sky. Full body centered, head around40% and feet around91% of
the image height. Preserve camera, identity, path, scale and matching shadows
across weather edits. No cartoon, illustration, plastic skin, 3D-render look,
text, logo or watermark.
